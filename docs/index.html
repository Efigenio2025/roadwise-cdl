SCHOOL BUS ROUTE

DRIVING STUDENTS TAKES MORE THAN A CDL.

For a Nebraska school bus designed for 16 or more people including the driver, the pupil-transport guide identifies a CDL with Passenger and School Bus endorsements. First-time P and S endorsements also require entry-level training. Employer, medical, driving-record, background, and pupil-transport requirements may apply.

The supplied Pupil Transportation Guide is dated August 2019. Treat it as supplemental material and confirm current Nebraska Department of Education requirements.
